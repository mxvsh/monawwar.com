faqs:
  - q: Where do the photos and videos go?
    a: Into a `Candid` folder in your own Google Drive, one subfolder per event. Candid only has access to the files and folders it creates, never the rest of your Drive.
  - q: Do guests need the app or an account?
    a: No. They scan the QR code, type the code or open the link in any mobile browser. The app is optional.
  - q: Does Candid keep a copy of my files?
    a: No. Uploads go from the guest's device straight to your Drive.
  - q: What if my Drive fills up?
    a: Link a second Drive to the event. Uploads fill the first Drive, then continue in the next.
  - q: Can I stop sharing an event?
    a: Yes. Rotate the code to lock out the old link, or delete the event. Files already in your Drive stay yours.
